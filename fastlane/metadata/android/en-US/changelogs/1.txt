Initial release of No head:
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Multi-Hop Mesh network
- Wi-Fi Aware (NAN) high-speed local transport
- End-to-end encrypted direct messaging (X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305)
- Public and Argon2id password-protected channels
- Store-and-Forward courier mailboxes
- Voice notes and Push-to-Talk (PTT)
- Coarse geohash channels
- Optional Nostr relays with Tor SOCKS5 proxy support
- SQLCipher-encrypted local storage with hardware Keystore backing
- Emergency Panic Wipe
